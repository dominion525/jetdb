Demo in the browser

jetdb (the WebAssembly build) opens an Access database (.mdb / .accdb) inside your browser and shows what is in it. A sample database is open to start with; you can open your own file too.

  • The sample is loaded from this site.
  • A file you open yourself stays in your browser. It is never uploaded.

PreviewThis page is being built. Tables, queries, modules, and macros show their contents; the other objects come next.
